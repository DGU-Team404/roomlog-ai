You are given an isometric wireframe projection of a 3D room mesh captured by an iPhone LiDAR scanner.

Your task is to generate a clean, stylized isometric thumbnail image of the room based on this wireframe.

Requirements:
- Isometric perspective view (45-degree angle from above-front)
- Clean architectural illustration style with soft colors
- Show room structure clearly: walls, floor, ceiling
- Light background (white or very light gray)
- Remove noise and artifacts from the scan
- Output should look like a polished architectural diagram or app icon

The input image is a matplotlib wireframe projection of the actual scanned room mesh. Use it as the structural reference to accurately represent the room layout and proportions.
